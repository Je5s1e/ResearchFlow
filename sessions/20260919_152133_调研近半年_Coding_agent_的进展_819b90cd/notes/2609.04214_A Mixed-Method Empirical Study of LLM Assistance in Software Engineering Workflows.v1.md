- 调查结果表明LLM被广泛采用且验证工作量大，同时两个年级组在约束密集场景下对LLM能力的感知存在差异。（页码：1）
  > Survey results indicate widespread LLM adoption and substantial veriﬁcation effort, alongside cohort differences in perceived LLM capability for constraint-heavy scenarios.

- 准实验进一步表明AI辅助改变了工作流结构，参与者常采用AI优先的任务进入、复制-转移集成和AI介导的调试，而非AI工作流更多依赖文档、先前模板和迭代试错精炼。（页码：1）
  > The quasi-experiment further shows that AI assistance changes workﬂow structure. For example, participants frequently adopt AI-ﬁrst task entry, copy–transfer integration, and AI-mediated debugging, whereas non-AI workﬂows rely more on documentation, prior templates, and iterative trial–error reﬁnement.

- 总体发现表明LLM辅助的收益取决于任务类型，并受专业知识和验证实践调节，而非仅由生成速度决定。（页码：1）
  > Overall, our ﬁndings suggest that the beneﬁts of LLM assistance are task-dependent and mediated by expertise and veriﬁcation practices, rather than by generation speed alone.
